Questions?

How does it work?
Grain analyzes your AI-generated text and rewrites it to sound more human. We replace robotic phrases, vary sentence structure, and add natural language patterns that human writers use.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Everything runs in your browser. We never store, access, or train on your text. Your writing stays completely private.
Does it work with all AI writing?
Works best with ChatGPT, Claude, and other LLM outputs. The more robotic the original, the better the transformation.
Can I cancel Pro anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no locked-in terms. Cancel your subscription anytime with one click.
What's the difference between intensities?
Light makes minimal changes. Standard balances naturalness with your original meaning. Aggressive rewrites heavily for maximum humanization.
